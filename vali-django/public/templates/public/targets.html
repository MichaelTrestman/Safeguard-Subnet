The same concern catalog, the same probe miners, the same audit rubric — applied against different variants of a target service at the same time. The grid below shows how a minor prompt-engineering change to a service shifts its safety posture.

Cells show average finding severity per probe — sum of finding severities divided by verified probe count. Weights by harm: a rare critical finding scores higher than a frequent low-severity one. Legacy evaluations (no provenance commitment) and unverified rows are excluded.
